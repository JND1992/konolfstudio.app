2. Was die App nicht tut

Die App übermittelt keine personenbezogenen Daten, weder an uns noch an Dritte. Insbesondere gibt es:

  • keine Registrierung und kein Benutzerkonto,
  • keine echte Werbung, keine Werbenetzwerke und keine Werbe-IDs,
  • keine echten In-App-Käufe (die Kauf-Knöpfe zeigen in dieser Testfassung nur einen Test-Kauf ohne Geld),
  • keine Analyse-, Statistik- oder Tracking-Dienste,
  • keinen Zugriff auf Standort, Kontakte, Kamera, Mikrofon, Fotos oder andere Dateien,
  • keine Mehrspieler-, Chat- oder Teilen-Funktionen.

Die App nutzt nur die Vibration deines Geräts (abschaltbar in den Einstellungen des Spiels).

3. Spielstand und Einstellungen auf deinem Gerät

Damit du weiterspielen kannst, speichert die App deinen Fortschritt (z. B. geschaffte Level, Gold, Diamanten, gekaufte Ausrüstung, Bauten) und deine Einstellungen (z. B. Ton, Sprache, Steuerung) lokal auf deinem Gerät. Wir haben darauf keinen Zugriff. Beim Deinstallieren der App oder über „Daten löschen“ in den App-Einstellungen von Android werden sie gelöscht.

Wenn auf deinem Gerät die automatische Android-Sicherung eingeschaltet ist, kann Android diese App-Daten in deinem Google-Konto sichern und bei einer Neuinstallation wiederherstellen. Diese Sicherung führt Google bzw. Android durch, nicht wir; du kannst sie in den Einstellungen deines Geräts abschalten.

Privacy Policy

for the app “Wallwarden” (Android) · Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short: Wallwarden runs offline. The app sends no data, has no tracking and no analytics, and we do not run any server of our own. Your game progress is stored on your device only. In the current test version, ads and purchases are only simulated: no real ads are loaded and nothing is charged.

2. What the app does not do

The app does not transmit any personal data, neither to us nor to third parties. In particular there is:

  • no registration and no user account,
  • no real advertising, no ad networks and no advertising IDs,
  • no real in-app purchases (in this test version the purchase buttons only show a test purchase without money),
  • no analytics, statistics or tracking services,
  • no access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or other files,
  • no multiplayer, chat or sharing features.

The app only uses your device's vibration (can be turned off in the game's settings).

3. Game progress and settings on your device

So that you can continue playing, the app stores your progress (e.g. completed levels, gold, diamonds, bought equipment, buildings) and your settings (e.g. sound, language, controls) locally on your device. We have no access to this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or use “Clear data” in Android's app settings.

If automatic Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up this app data to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall. This backup is performed by Google/Android, not by us, and can be turned off in your device settings.

4. Testing via Google Play

Wallwarden is first distributed as a closed test on Google Play. Google Play invites testers by their email address or Google account; this list is managed in the Google Play Console. We use these addresses only to run the test and not for marketing. If you send us feedback by email or through Google Play's feedback feature, we process your email address or name and the content of your message to improve the game and to reply to you (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in improving the app; in Switzerland the principles of the FADP).

How to delete your data:
  • Game progress on your device: uninstall the app or choose “Clear data” for Wallwarden in Android's settings.
  • Messages to us and the tester list: send an email to support@konolfstudio.app with the subject “Delete Wallwarden” and tell us the email address you use for the test or wrote to us from. Within one month we delete your messages to us, remove you from the tester list if you wish, and confirm this to you by email. Without a request, we delete feedback emails once we no longer need them, no later than 12 months after the end of the test.
There is no user account with us that would need to be deleted.

5. Google Play Store

When you download the app from Google Play, Google processes data under its own responsibility (e.g. your Google account and download information). We have no influence on this. Statistics we see in the Google Play Console are aggregated and do not identify individual people. The Google Privacy Policy applies.

6. Children

Wallwarden is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not collect any data from children. Children should not write to us without their parents. If we learn that a child has sent us personal data, we delete it.

7. Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) or – if you live in the EU – the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, FDPIC; in the EU the authority of your country of residence). Apart from messages you send us yourself (section 4), we do not store any personal data about you. If you have any questions, contact us at the email address above.

This privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the page and protect it from attacks. The page itself uses no cookies, no scripts and no embedded third-party content. GitHub's privacy policy: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If the app changes in a way that affects how data is processed (for example through real ads or real purchases via Google Play), we will update this privacy policy beforehand. The version published here applies.

2. Welche Daten verarbeitet die App?

Die App erhebt, speichert oder übermittelt keine personenbezogenen Daten an uns oder an Dritte. Insbesondere gibt es:

  • keine Registrierung und kein Benutzerkonto,
  • keine Werbung und keine Werbe-IDs,

  • keine Analyse-, Statistik-, Absturzbericht- oder Tracking-Dienste,
  • keinen Zugriff auf Standort, Kontakte, Kamera, Mikrofon, Fotos oder andere Dateien,

Die App fordert keine Internet-Berechtigung an und sendet selbst keine Daten über das Internet. Für den Kauf der Vollversion spricht sie nur mit der Google-Play-Store-App auf deinem Gerät (Berechtigung „Google Play Billing“).

3. Spielstand, Rangliste und Einstellungen auf deinem Gerät

Damit du weiterspielen kannst, speichert die App deinen Spielstand (je Spielvariante: Klassisch, Wirtschaft, Großhof), die lokale Rangliste mit den Namen, die du dort selbst einträgst, und deine Einstellungen (z. B. Ton an/aus, zuletzt gewählte Spielvariante, die verbrauchte Demo-Zeit und ob die Vollversion freigeschaltet ist) nur lokal auf deinem Gerät. Wir haben darauf keinen Zugriff. Über „Neues Spiel“ im Startmenü kannst du einen Spielstand löschen; beim Deinstallieren der App werden alle diese Daten gelöscht.

Wenn auf deinem Gerät die automatische Android-Sicherung („Google One / Android-Backup“) eingeschaltet ist, kann Android diese App-Daten in deinem Google-Konto sichern und bei einer Neuinstallation wiederherstellen. Diese Sicherung wird von Google bzw. Android durchgeführt, nicht von uns, und kann in den Einstellungen deines Geräts abgeschaltet werden.

2. What data does the app process?

The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to us or to third parties. In particular there is:

  • no registration and no user account,
  • no advertising and no advertising IDs,
  • no subscriptions and no purchases other than the one-time full version (see section 4),
  • no analytics, statistics, crash reporting or tracking services,
  • no access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or other files,
  • no chat or sharing features.

The app does not request the internet permission and does not send any data over the internet itself. To buy the full version it only talks to the Google Play Store app on your device (“Google Play Billing” permission).

3. Game progress, leaderboard and settings on your device

So that you can continue playing, the app stores your game progress (per game mode: Classic, Economy, Big Estate), the local leaderboard with the names you enter yourself, and your settings (e.g. sound on/off, last selected game mode, the demo time used and whether the full version is unlocked) only locally on your device. We have no access to this data. You can delete a save with “New game” in the start menu; uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

If automatic Android backup (“Google One / Android backup”) is turned on for your device, Android may back up this app data to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall. This backup is performed by Google/Android, not by us, and can be turned off in your device settings.

4. Buying the full version via Google Play

Little Farmstand can be played for free as a demo (Classic up to 50 %, Economy and Big Estate for 10 minutes each). The full version is a one-time in-app purchase without a subscription. Purchase and payment are handled entirely by Google Play (Google Ireland Limited or Google LLC) under their own responsibility; payment data and your Google account are processed only by Google. The app uses the Google Play Billing Library and only asks Google Play whether the full version has been bought with your Google account (at start, when buying and with “Restore purchase”). For accounting, Google provides us with the usual purchase details (e.g. order number, date, price and country), but neither your name nor your payment data. No other third-party SDKs are included.

5. Children

The app does not collect personal data, including from children. Purchases are only possible through the Google Play purchase dialog, which can be protected by Google Play's parental controls and purchase approval settings (e.g. Family Link). The app shows no ads and does not pressure anyone to buy.

6. Google Play Store

When you download the app from Google Play, Google processes data under its own responsibility (e.g. your Google account and download information). We have no influence on this. Statistics we see in the Google Play Console are aggregated and do not identify individual people. The Google Privacy Policy applies.

7. Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) or – if you live in the EU – the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, FDPIC; in the EU the authority of your country of residence). As we do not store any personal data about you, we usually cannot provide or delete any data – you can delete your game progress at any time yourself. If you have any questions, contact us at the email address above.

9. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects how data is processed, we will update this privacy policy. The version published here applies.

2. Welche Daten verarbeitet die App?

Die App erhebt, speichert oder übermittelt keine personenbezogenen Daten an uns oder an Dritte. Insbesondere gibt es:

  • keine Registrierung und kein Benutzerkonto,
  • keine Werbung und keine Werbe-IDs,
  • keine In-App-Käufe,
  • keine Analyse-, Statistik- oder Tracking-Dienste und keine eingebundenen Fremd-SDKs,
  • keinen Zugriff auf Standort, Kontakte, Kamera, Mikrofon, Fotos oder andere Dateien,
  • keine Mehrspieler-, Chat- oder Teilen-Funktionen.

Gespielt wird vollständig offline. Die App ruft keine Internet-Adressen auf und sendet keine Daten über das Internet.

3. Spielstand und Einstellungen auf deinem Gerät

Damit du weiterspielen kannst, speichert die App deinen Spielfortschritt (z. B. freigeschaltete Level und Karten, Sterne, Ruhm, den Stand deiner Burg und des Tutorials) und deine Einstellungen (z. B. Ton an/aus) nur lokal auf deinem Gerät. Wir haben darauf keinen Zugriff. Beim Deinstallieren der App oder über „Daten löschen“ in den App-Einstellungen von Android werden alle diese Daten gelöscht.

Wenn auf deinem Gerät die automatische Android-Sicherung („Google One / Android-Backup“) eingeschaltet ist, kann Android diese App-Daten in deinem Google-Konto sichern und bei einer Neuinstallation wiederherstellen. Diese Sicherung wird von Google bzw. Android durchgeführt, nicht von uns, und kann in den Einstellungen deines Geräts abgeschaltet werden.

  1. Schreib eine E-Mail an support@konolfstudio.app mit dem Betreff „Löschen Mortarkeep“.

Privacy Policy

for the app “Mortarkeep” (Android) · Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: Mortarkeep does not collect any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no ads, no purchases, no tracking and no analytics. It does not connect to our servers or anyone else's, and your game progress stays on your device. Data is only processed by Google itself when you get the app from Google Play (section 5).

2. What data does the app process?

The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to us or to third parties. In particular there is:

  • no registration and no user account,
  • no advertising and no advertising IDs,
  • no in-app purchases,
  • no analytics, statistics or tracking services and no embedded third-party SDKs,
  • no access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or other files,
  • no multiplayer, chat or sharing features.

The game is played entirely offline. The app does not call any internet addresses and does not send any data over the internet.

3. Game progress and settings on your device

So that you can continue playing, the app stores your progress (e.g. unlocked levels and maps, stars, fame, the state of your castle and of the tutorial) and your settings (e.g. sound on/off) only locally on your device. We have no access to this data. All of it is deleted when you uninstall the app or use “Clear data” in Android's app settings.

If automatic Android backup (“Google One / Android backup”) is turned on for your device, Android may back up this app data to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall. This backup is performed by Google/Android, not by us, and can be turned off in your device settings.

4. Testing via Google Play

Mortarkeep is first distributed as a test on Google Play. For the closed test, Google Play invites testers by their email address or Google account; this list is managed in the Google Play Console and participation runs through Google. We use these addresses only to run the test and not for marketing. If you send us feedback by email or through Google Play's feedback feature, we process your email address or name and the content of your message to improve the game and to reply to you (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in improving the app; in Switzerland the principles of the FADP). The app itself sends nothing, not even in the test version.

How to have your data deleted:
  1. Send an email to support@konolfstudio.app with the subject “Delete Mortarkeep”.
  2. Tell us the email address you use for the test or wrote to us from.
  3. Within one month we delete your messages to us, remove you from the tester list if you wish, and confirm this to you by email.
Without a request, we delete feedback emails once we no longer need them, no later than 12 months after the end of the test. Game progress and settings are only stored on your device; you delete them yourself by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android's settings (section 3). There is no user account that would need to be deleted.

5. Google Play Store

When you download the app from Google Play, Google processes data under its own responsibility (e.g. your Google account and download information). We have no influence on this. Statistics we see in the Google Play Console are aggregated and do not identify individual people. The Google Privacy Policy applies.

6. Children

As the app does not collect any personal data, no data of children is collected either. Children should not write to us without their parents. If we learn that a child has sent us personal data, we delete it.

7. Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) or – if you live in the EU – the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, FDPIC; in the EU the authority of your country of residence). Apart from messages you send us yourself (section 4), we do not store any personal data about you. If you have any questions, contact us at the email address above.

This privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the page and protect it from attacks. The page itself uses no cookies, no scripts and no embedded third-party content. GitHub's privacy policy: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

9. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects how data is processed (for example through a full-version purchase via Google Play), we will update this privacy policy beforehand. The version published here applies.

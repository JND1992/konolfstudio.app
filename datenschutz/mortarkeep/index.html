3. Spielstand und Einstellungen auf deinem Gerät

Damit du weiterspielen kannst, speichert die App deinen Spielfortschritt (z. B. freigeschaltete Level und Karten, Sterne, Ruhm, den Stand deiner Burg und des Tutorials) und deine Einstellungen (z. B. Ton an/aus) lokal auf deinem Gerät. Dazu kommt eine zufällig erzeugte Kennung dieser Installation, mit der die App erkennt, ob eine Sicherung von diesem oder einem anderen Gerät stammt; sie ist keine Geräte- oder Werbekennung und lässt keinen Rückschluss auf dich zu. Wenn auf deinem Gerät die automatische Android-Sicherung („Google One / Android-Backup“) eingeschaltet ist, kann Android diese App-Daten in deinem Google-Konto sichern und bei einer Neuinstallation wiederherstellen. Diese Sicherung wird von Google bzw. Android durchgeführt, nicht von uns, und kann in den Einstellungen deines Geräts abgeschaltet werden.

4. Google Play Games: Anmeldung und Spielstand-Sicherung

Mortarkeep verwendet die Google Play Games Services der Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Irland (Mutterkonzern: Google LLC, USA), eingebunden über das Programmpaket „Google Play Games Plugin for Unity“.

  • Anmeldung: Bist du auf deinem Gerät bei Google Play Games angemeldet, meldet sich die App beim Start automatisch an; sonst nur, wenn du im Pausenmenü „Mit Google Play anmelden“ wählst. Die App erhält dabei deinen Play-Games-Anzeigenamen (er wird im Pausenmenü angezeigt) und eine spielbezogene Spieler-ID von Google. Deine E-Mail-Adresse oder dein Passwort erhält die App nicht.
  • Spielstand-Sicherung („Gespeicherte Spiele“): Solange du angemeldet bist, lädt die App deinen Spielstand aus Abschnitt 3 (Fortschritt, Ton-Einstellung, Zeitpunkt der Sicherung, Ruhm und die zufällige Installations-Kennung) regelmäßig, beim Verlassen der App und auf deinen Wunsch („Jetzt sichern“) in deinen Google-Konto-Speicher hoch (versteckter App-Ordner in Google Drive). Beim Start und mit „Laden“ liest sie ihn wieder, z. B. um deinen Fortschritt auf ein neues Gerät zu übernehmen. Findet sie einen neueren Stand von einem anderen Gerät, fragt sie dich vorher.

Für die Daten deines Play-Games-Profils und den Betrieb des Dienstes ist Google selbst verantwortlich; es gelten die Datenschutzerklärung von Google und die Nutzungsbedingungen von Google Play.

Die Anmeldung ist freiwillig; du kannst ohne sie vollständig spielen, dann bleibt der Spielstand nur auf deinem Gerät. Die automatische Anmeldung kannst du in den Einstellungen von Google Play Games abschalten. for the app “Mortarkeep” (Android) · Last updated: 9 November 2026

If you are signed in with Google Play Games, the app also backs it up to your Google account so you can continue on another device or after reinstalling (section 4). This data is stored by Google; we cannot see it ourselves.

2. What the app does not do

The app does not transmit any personal data to us. In particular there is:

  • no registration and no user account with us (sign-in only happens through Google Play Games, section 4),
  • no advertising and no advertising IDs,
  • no in-app purchases,
  • no analytics, statistics or tracking services,
  • no access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or other files,
  • no multiplayer, chat or sharing features.

The game itself runs offline. The app's only internet connection is to Google Play Games (section 4); without signing in, the app sends nothing.

3. Game progress and settings on your device

So that you can continue playing, the app stores your progress (e.g. unlocked levels and maps, stars, fame, the state of your castle and of the tutorial) and your settings (e.g. sound on/off) locally on your device, together with a randomly generated ID for this installation that lets the app tell whether a backup came from this device or another one. It is not a device or advertising identifier and does not identify you. We have no access to this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or use “Clear data” in Android's app settings.

If automatic Android backup (“Google One / Android backup”) is turned on for your device, Android may back up this app data to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall. This backup is performed by Google/Android, not by us, and can be turned off in your device settings.

4. Google Play Games: sign-in and cloud save

Mortarkeep uses Google Play Games Services provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (parent company: Google LLC, USA), integrated through the “Google Play Games Plugin for Unity” library.

  • Sign-in: If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the app signs in automatically at start; otherwise only when you choose “Mit Google Play anmelden” (sign in with Google Play) in the pause menu. The app receives your Play Games display name (shown in the pause menu) and a game-specific player ID from Google. The app does not receive your email address or password.
  • Cloud save (“Saved Games”): While you are signed in, the app uploads your game state from section 3 (progress, sound setting, time of the backup, fame and the random installation ID) to your Google account storage (a hidden app folder in Google Drive) at regular intervals, when you leave the app and when you choose “Jetzt sichern” (back up now). At start and with “Laden” (load) it reads it back, e.g. to move your progress to a new device. If it finds a newer state from another device, it asks you first.

This data is stored on Google's servers, including outside Switzerland and the EU (e.g. in the USA); Google bases such transfers on standard contractual clauses or the EU-US/Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework. We do not run any server and have no access to your saved games – only the app on a device signed in with the same Google account can read them. Google itself is responsible for your Play Games profile data and for operating the service; the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service apply.

Signing in is optional; you can play the full game without it, in which case your progress stays on your device only. You can turn off automatic sign-in in the Google Play Games settings. The legal basis is providing the backup feature you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; in Switzerland the principles of the FADP).

5. Testing via Google Play

Mortarkeep is first distributed as a test on Google Play. For the closed test, Google Play invites testers by their email address or Google account; this list is managed in the Google Play Console and participation runs through Google. We use these addresses only to run the test and not for marketing. If you send us feedback by email or through Google Play's feedback feature, we process your email address or name and the content of your message to improve the game and to reply to you (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in improving the app; in Switzerland the principles of the FADP).

How to delete your data:
  • Game progress on your device: uninstall the app or choose “Clear data” for Mortarkeep in Android's settings.
  • Cloud save in Google Play Games: open Google Drive → Settings → “Manage apps”, then for Mortarkeep choose “Options” → “Delete hidden app data”. Alternatively, in the Google Play Games app under “Settings” you can delete the data of individual games or your whole Play Games profile. As we have no access to this data, only you can delete it; we are happy to help.
  • Messages to us and the tester list: send an email to support@konolfstudio.app with the subject “Delete Mortarkeep” and tell us the email address you use for the test or wrote to us from. Within one month we delete your messages to us, remove you from the tester list if you wish, and confirm this to you by email. Without a request, we delete feedback emails once we no longer need them, no later than 12 months after the end of the test.
There is no user account with us that would need to be deleted.

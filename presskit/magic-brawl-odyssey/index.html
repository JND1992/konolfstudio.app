About the game

Magic Brawl Odyssey is a side-scrolling fantasy brawler with real action RPG progression. Players pick a hero, chain combos, block, parry at the perfect moment, roll and dodge, and fight through wave after wave of enemies that attack as a coordinated group, until a world boss blocks the path.

The game is built for co-op on phones: friends join with a 6-character room code or ask to join a public table, and AI heroes fill every free spot. If a player drops out, the AI takes over their hero until they come back. Everything can also be played solo and offline.

Key features

  • Online co-op for up to 8 players: room codes or public tables (the host decides), shared lives and revives, saved rooms. No text or voice chat. Joining is always free, and co-op is ad-free.
  • 15 heroes with their own fighting styles and 4 special abilities each, from the sturdy Paladin and the swift Shadowblade to the Necromancer, the Druid and the gadget-loving Tinkerer.
  • 7 worlds, 175 levels: 25 levels per world, a boss fight every fifth level and a different objective in each world: protect the World Tree, escape the lava, escort a caravan, destroy a robot factory.
  • 77 enemy types including elite mini-bosses like the Troll, the Werewolf and the Frost Giant, and 10 elements with weaknesses and resistances.
  • Action RPG progression: hero level 100, 11 attributes, loot for 6 gear slots, a forge that turns 4 items into 1 of the next tier. Gear is found or forged, never sold.
  • Endless mode for every world, solo or in co-op, three difficulties, 25 cosmetic awards and stats after every level.
  • Fair unlocking: World 1 and three heroes are free, and every locked hero can be tried in training. Stats, gear and coins are never for sale.

About Konolf Studio

Konolf Studio is a one-person studio making mobile games for Android. Magic Brawl Odyssey is its first big release. I designed the game, its heroes, enemies and worlds, and tested every build on my own phone. The code was written with the help of an AI coding assistant. Every world has its own music track, composed by the game's code while it runs.
